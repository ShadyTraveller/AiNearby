Questions

What counts as an “AI service”?

Any hands-on, local help built on AI tools: someone who sets up a chatbot for your shop, a tutor who teaches with AI, a studio making AI-assisted headshots or video, a technician who installs voice assistants and smart cameras, or a consultant who automates your paperwork.

Does AI Nearby book or charge anything?

No. AI Nearby seeks connection, and is not a marketplace. Searches open live map results, and you deal with the provider directly, no booking fees, no commission.

Why do results open in a map?

Local providers open, close, and move all the time. A live map search always shows current businesses, hours, reviews, and directions for your exact area.

Is my location shared with AI Nearby?

No. If you tap “Near me,” your browser uses your location only to build the map search on your device. Nothing is sent to or stored by us. We're just here to help.
